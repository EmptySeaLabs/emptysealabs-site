Holy Moly

Privacy

Holy Moly doesn't collect anything about you. No accounts, no tracking, no ads. Your progress never leaves your iPhone.

Last updated 14 November 2026

What this policy covers

This policy covers Holy Moly, the game for iPhone made by Empty Sea Labs. Our website has its own short policy: see the website privacy page.

What we collect

Nothing. The game has no accounts and no sign-in. It never asks for your name, email, contacts, location, photos, camera or microphone. It contains no ads, no analytics or tracking code, and no code from other companies. It doesn't connect to the internet at all.

What stays on your iPhone

The game saves which level you've reached and your best scores, so you can pick up where you left off. That information is stored only on your device. It is never sent to us or to anyone else, and deleting the game deletes it.

What Apple may share with us

If you've chosen to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone's settings, Apple gives us combined figures such as how many people downloaded the game and how often it crashed. Those figures don't identify you. You can turn this off in Settings, under Privacy & Security, then Analytics & Improvements.

Children

Holy Moly collects no personal information from anyone, children included.

If you email us

If you write to us for help, we receive your email address and whatever you include in your message. We use it only to reply and to fix the problem, never for marketing, and we don't sell or share it. You can ask us to delete your messages at any time.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.

Contact

Questions about privacy: support@emptysealabs.com
