Support

Stuck, found a bug, or have an idea for the game? Here's how it plays, answers to common questions, and how to reach us.

Get in touch

Email support@emptysealabs.com. We're a one-person studio and reply as soon as we can. It helps to say which iPhone you have, which version of iOS it runs, and what happened just before the problem.

How to play

You're a hole on a golf course. Drag anywhere on the screen to move. Anything that fits drops in once all of it is over the hole, and the hole grows as it eats. A round is 18 holes, after a warm-up at the clubhouse. Each hole gives you a short list of challenges, as many as its par: collect golf balls, round up the carts, swallow a landmark. Clear the list and the flag opens; sink the flag to finish the hole. Every try is a stroke and lasts 60 seconds. If time runs out you go again, a little bigger, and whatever you already cleared stays cleared. You get up to six strokes a hole, and fewer is better. The badge at the top left shows your size.

Boosters

The three buttons at the bottom right of the screen are boosters: a Magnet that pulls things in from further away, a Mega Hole that makes you bigger, and a Time Freeze that stops the clock. Tap one to use it for a few seconds. The number on each button is how many you have left. You get some free on the warm-up and your first three holes.

Bonus rounds

After each hole you get a short bonus round in the woods. Grab enough golf balls before the clock runs out and you pick a prize: a Mulligan, which takes one stroke off your card, or one of each booster.

The full course

The warm-up and holes 1 to 3 are free. One purchase unlocks holes 4 to 18 and every course we add later. It's a single payment, not a subscription.

I bought the full course but it's locked

Open Settings (the gear on the start screen) and tap Restore purchases. That brings your purchase back on a new iPhone, or after reinstalling, as long as you're signed in with the same Apple Account. For a refund, use Apple's Report a Problem page; we can't refund App Store purchases ourselves.

The game paused itself

If a call comes in or you switch apps in the middle of a stroke, the game freezes the clock. When you come back, tap Carry on and the stroke picks up exactly where it stopped.

There's no sound

The game follows your iPhone's silent setting. Turn off Silent Mode (the switch or Action button on the side, or in Control Centre) and turn the volume up.

Something is "too big"

If the hole gets under something it can't swallow yet, the thing wedges for a moment and slows you down, then pops free. Eat smaller things first and come back when you've grown.

Where is my progress kept?

Your level and best scores are saved on your iPhone only. There's no account, so progress doesn't move between devices, and deleting the game deletes it.

Which iPhones does it run on?

iPhone 11 and later, running iOS 26 or newer.

Privacy
