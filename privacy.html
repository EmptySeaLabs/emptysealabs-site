Privacy

This website doesn't track you. No analytics, no cookies, no ads, no sign-ups. Here's the whole picture.

Last updated 17 October 2026

What this policy covers

This policy covers emptysealabs.com, the studio website run by Empty Sea Labs. Each of our apps has its own privacy policy that explains what that app does with your data. For Viewography, see the Viewography privacy policy.

What we collect

Nothing. The site is plain HTML and CSS. It sets no cookies, runs no analytics or tracking scripts, loads no third-party fonts, embeds or ads, and has no forms or accounts.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web server, GitHub may log technical details of each request, such as your IP address, for security and to keep the service running. We don't receive or use those logs. You can read how GitHub handles this in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

If you email us

If you write to us, we receive your email address and whatever you include in your message. We use it only to reply and to help with your question, never for marketing, and we don't sell or share it. You can ask us to delete your messages at any time.

Links to other sites

This site links to our app websites and to a few other services. Those sites have their own privacy practices, which this policy doesn't cover.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
